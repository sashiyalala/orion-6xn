>[!definition] Objeto Herbig-Haro (HH)
> Objetos nebulosos que aparecen en estrellas jóvenes. Las estrellas lanzan chorros de gas ionizado (i.e., cargado) que salen disparados en el mismo eje de rotación de la estrella. Estos objetos son el resultado de la interacción entre dichos rayos y las nubes de gas y polvo estelar alrededor de la estrella.

- La forma en que se crean (la interacción entre gas estelar y chorros de gas ionizado) solo resulta en ondas visibles
	- De hecho no emiten infrarrojo
> Both [George Herbig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Herbig "George Herbig") and [Guillermo Haro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guillermo_Haro "Guillermo Haro") made independent observations of several of these objects in the [Orion Nebula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orion_Nebula "Orion Nebula") during the 1940s. Herbig also looked at Burnham's Nebula and found it displayed an unusual [electromagnetic spectrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stellar_spectrum "Stellar spectrum"), with prominent [emission lines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emission_line "Emission line") of [hydrogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrogen "Hydrogen"), [sulfur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sulfur "Sulfur") and [oxygen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxygen "Oxygen"). Haro found that all the objects of this type were invisible in infrared light
