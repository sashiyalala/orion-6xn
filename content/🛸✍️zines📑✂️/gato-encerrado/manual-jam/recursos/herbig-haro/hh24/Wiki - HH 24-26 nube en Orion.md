> **HH 24-26** is a [molecular cloud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molecular_cloud "Molecular cloud") and [star-forming region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_formation "Star formation") containing the [Herbig-Haro objects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbig%E2%80%93Haro_object "Herbig–Haro object") HH 24, HH 25 and HH 26. This region contains the highest concentration of [astrophysical jets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrophysical_jet "Astrophysical jet") known anywhere in the sky.

Se halla en la estrella Orión B, ver [[Location of HH 24 in Orion]] para visualizar exactamente dónde está.
