Popularly known by its Japanese name, a *shitajiki* or **pencil board**, is a piece of, usually, harder but flexible material (plastic, carboard...) that is used to put under your writing area in notebooks to protect the pages underneath from creasing, marks...etc.

>[!question] 
>Not sure how to translate it in Spanish. ¿Plantilla de escritura? Así lo llaman en algunas tiendas españolas...

This one in particular is a transparent A5-size model from the Japanese brand *Hobonichi*. It came for free with the HON books (a variation of the brand's popular yearly journals), one of which I had ordered for 2025.

It also has a pen loop that works as a bookmark too. I'm not a big fan, as it adds volume.
